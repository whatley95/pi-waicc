# Skill sources

The 14 `skills/wai-*` packages and their references were vendored from Whatley's
pi-yoowai 1.3.0 skills collection on 2026-10-16.
That package identifies the author as whatley.xyz and its license as MIT. Its skill
frontmatter license declarations are retained. No runtime dependency on that checkout
or on pi-yoowai is introduced; the original checkout is unchanged.

WAICC adaptations replace legacy WAI memory/delivery tool references and remove the
requirement for `wai_design_ref`. All other domain guidance and linked references
are retained. `wai-skill-design/LICENSE` contains the upstream MIT notice for
Emil Kowalski's design guidance and is included in installed/exported packages.

`skills/waicc-workflow` is authored for pi-waicc and covered by the root MIT license.
